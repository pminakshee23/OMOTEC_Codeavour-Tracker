OMOTEC Codeavour 8.0 – Competition Tracking Dashboard
One dashboard to plan, track, and review team preparation across all three Codeavour tracks.

Dashboard: https://pminakshee23.github.io/OMOTEC_Codeavour-Tracker/
About the dashboard
The OMOTEC Codeavour 8.0 Competition Tracking Dashboard is a central workspace for managing competition teams, recording training progress, monitoring submissions, and coordinating preparation activities. It helps trainers and track leads maintain consistent updates and gives the management team a consolidated view of readiness across branches.
Competition tracks
Track	Focus	Dashboard highlights
Track 1 – Innovation	Project ideas and prototype development	Idea Bank, idea review and assignment, project approval, documentation and presentation readiness
Track 2 – Robo Line Follower	Robot development and challenge practice	Milestone tracking, robot approval, practice timer and challenge records
Track 3 – Robo Soccer	Robot development and match preparation	Robot 1 / Robot 2 / backup tracking, team progress cards, match scoreboard, match history and leaderboard


Common features for every track
Team Management
Create and organize teams by track, category, branch, trainer, and lead. Authorized administrators can update team names and manage archived teams.
Weekly Updates
A shared process for Track 1, Track 2, and Track 3. Trainers record progress, work completed, challenges, next steps, and remarks so leads can review team activity regularly.
Inventory Management
A shared process for all teams, covering inventory or requirement submissions, submission dates, follow-up status, and previous entries. This helps track pending items and support requests.
Progress Monitoring
View each team's preparation status, completed activities, pending work, and important follow-ups. Use the dashboard to identify teams needing attention.
Approvals and Readiness
Record applicable project or robot approvals and monitor preparation milestones and competition deliverables.
Reports and Records
Review available team updates, activity histories, and track-specific records. Some sections support CSV import or export.
User roles
Role	Main responsibility
Super Admin	Overall coordination, team and account management, and dashboard-wide monitoring
Track Lead	Review assigned track, guide trainers, monitor progress, and follow up on pending items
Trainer	Maintain team details, submit weekly updates, record inventory requirements, and update training progress
Observation Team	View progress and records for monitoring and review


A Super Admin may also be assigned as a team's project lead.
Typical weekly workflow
1. Open the dashboard and select the relevant track or team.
2. Review the team's current progress and pending activities.
3. Add or update the Weekly Update for the team.
4. Review Inventory Management and record any requirements or submission changes.
5. Update track-specific milestones, approvals, documentation, or match/practice records as applicable.
6. Track Leads review updates, follow up on delays, and coordinate next steps.
Track-specific overview
Track 1 – Innovation: Maintain project ideas in the Idea Bank, review and assign ideas, track prototype/project approval, and monitor documentation, poster, presentation slides, video demonstration, and presentation/video script readiness.

Track 2 – Robo Line Follower: Monitor robot-building milestones, record approvals, and track practice sessions and challenge performance.

Track 3 – Robo Soccer: Monitor the preparation of two main robots and a backup, use progress cards to review readiness, manage practice or competition matches, and review match results and rankings.
Getting started
1. Visit the Codeavour 8.0 Dashboard.
2. Sign in using the account details provided by your dashboard coordinator.
3. Open your assigned teams and review their information.
4. Start updating team progress, weekly reports, and inventory records.
5. Share suggestions and report any issues to the dashboard coordinator so the workflow can be improved.
Purpose
The dashboard is intended to make Codeavour preparation organized, visible, consistent, and easier to coordinate across branches and tracks. It is an evolving internal tool, and feedback from the team will help improve its usefulness.
